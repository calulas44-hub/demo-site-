# Passion MD — Season 2, Episode 1: 「義賊」 The Righteous Thieves

Production prompts, English version of `saison2-episode1-prompts.md`. This episode merges
episodes 1 to 3 of the season 2 plan (*The Double*, *The Righteous Thieves*, *Return*).
See `saison2.md` for the full season direction.

The prompts are written in English because most generators (Kling, Hailuo, Vidu, Higgsfield,
Seedance) follow English more reliably. The spoken lines stay in Japanese, as in season 1;
the subtitle translations are given after each clip so they can be typed into CapCut in
French or in English.

- **Target length:** about 8 min 30.
- **Volume:** 52 clips of 8 to 12 s, in 12 sequences.
- **Generators:** Kling for the fights, Hailuo or Vidu as backup. Wan2GP locally for the
  collapsing building if the online generators refuse it.
- **Editing:** CapCut. Every piece of text (title, subtitles, graffiti, text message, title
  cards) is added in the edit, never generated.
